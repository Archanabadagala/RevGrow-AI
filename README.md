**RevGrow AI**
**AI-Powered Merchant Growth & Agentic Commerce Platform**
RevGrow AI helps merchants identify underperforming products and discover opportunities to increase sales.
At the core is **Rega**, an AI growth agent that analyzes product performance and recommends targeted actions such as limited-time discounts.
**How Rega Works**
Rega identifies products that are selling below their expected performance.
**Example**
**Wireless Buds**
- Sold: 7 units
- Expected: 20 units
- Sales Gap: 13 units
**Rega recommends:**  
10% discount for 24 hours.
The merchant can **Approve** or **Reject** the recommendation.

**Safety & Approval**
Rega does not automatically perform money-related actions.
Every recommendation requires merchant approval before the action is taken.
The decision is then recorded in the Activity Log.
Detect → Recommend → Approve → Execute → Record
**Test Payment**
RevGrow AI includes a test payment simulation through the Node.js/Express backend.
It demonstrates the customer payment flow without processing real money.
No real payments are processed in the current MVP.
